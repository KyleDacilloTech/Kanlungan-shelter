How this would really work

The accessibility flags on each shelter, like ramps and gender-neutral facilities, come straight from our interviews: evacuees like Ludy, who uses a wheelchair, and Ping, who found binary-gendered spaces left them with nowhere to go, described these exact gaps.

This prototype now uses JavaScript for the interactive parts: choosing your barangay recalculates and re-sorts distances, and Coordinator mode lets you adjust a shelter's headcount, with the badge and bar updating instantly and saving on this device. What's still simulated: distances come from a fixed area table, not live GPS, and headcount changes only save on your own browser, they do not yet sync to anyone else's phone. A real deployment would use real coordinates and a shared backend so every device sees the same numbers.
